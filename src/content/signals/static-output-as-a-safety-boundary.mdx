[Astro's static and on-demand rendering guide](https://docs.astro.build/en/guides/on-demand-rendering/) is useful because it makes the delivery boundary explicit: content can be built and inspected before it is served.

For this portfolio, that boundary supports a public-content audit and keeps the published site separate from local sources and operational state. The resource matters as a design reference, not as a claim that static output removes the need for editorial review.
