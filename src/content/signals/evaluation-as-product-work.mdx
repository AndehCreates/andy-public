Reliability has a user-facing shape. A confidence label, a missing-input state, and a next-evidence cue can help someone decide what to inspect instead of receiving a polished result with no visible limits.

This relates to the work because decision-support interfaces need to expose uncertainty as part of the product, not leave it buried in implementation notes. The useful question is not whether an output looks complete; it is whether a person can understand what would change their mind.
