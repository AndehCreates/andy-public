Local infrastructure is most useful when its state can be understood without relying on a person remembering every recovery step. A concise status surface and a durable handoff record make maintenance calmer and failures easier to revisit.

This note deliberately stays at the pattern level. It omits addresses, host names, inventories, access methods, and topology; the public lesson is about inspectable recovery, not a deployable infrastructure recipe.
