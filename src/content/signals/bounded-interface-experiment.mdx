Small experiments can expose a system's behavior earlier than a fully composed experience. The useful constraint is to keep inputs, output, and the person's next choice visible enough to inspect together.

This connects to the wider work by treating interaction as an evaluation surface: a prototype can reveal ambiguity, awkward control boundaries, or missing feedback before those assumptions become architecture.
