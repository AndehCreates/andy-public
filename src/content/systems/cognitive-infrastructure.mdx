## What this system is for

Cognitive Infrastructure is a public-safe projection of how context, boundaries, coordination, execution, and technical records can work together. It describes distinct responsibilities and deliberate handoffs rather than a live topology, deployment, or unattended operating model.

## How to read the Observatory

Read from human direction through the layers below. Each layer has a different responsibility and authority boundary. The goal is not to centralize judgment in software; it is to make useful work easier to inspect, recover, and improve while consequential authority stays with people.

## What remains outside this view

This field guide intentionally omits private infrastructure, endpoints, source locations, credentials, operational logs, and internal availability details. A visible component may be explained without being offered as a public drill-down when its public project material is not yet available.
