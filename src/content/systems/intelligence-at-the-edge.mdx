## How to read this map

This map is a design lens for intelligence close to the people and devices it serves. A local workload can combine models, device input, and sensor observations, but the useful boundary is still the person who can inspect state, understand limits, and choose the next action. The text explains the map without depending on the visual.

## Map legend

- **Nodes** name responsibilities that may be combined or separated in a real implementation.
- **Arrows** show a direction of information or intentional control, not a network connection.
- **Human review** is the decision boundary for interpreting uncertain observations or model output.

## What this connects

The connected public work explores inspectable local state and deterministic interactive behavior. The map intentionally omits host names, addresses, device inventories, topology, and operational access details; it is a public design view, not an infrastructure diagram.
