## How to read this map

This map follows the decisions that make AI-assisted work dependable. Human judgment sets the boundary; durable context preserves what matters between sessions; orchestration requests bounded work; evaluation checks what happened; and recovery keeps uncertain work legible instead of treating it as silently complete. The text below describes the relationships independently of the diagram.

## Map legend

- **Nodes** are responsibilities, not a claim about a particular runtime or deployment.
- **Arrows** show an accountable handoff of context, evidence, or a decision.
- **Recovery paths** keep blocked or inconclusive work available for review and re-entry.

## What this connects

Reliable AI work depends on distributed authority rather than a single system declaring itself complete. The connected public work explores that posture through explicit lifecycle state, evaluation boundaries, and a recoverable handoff record. It does not claim unattended operation or a universal automation pattern.
