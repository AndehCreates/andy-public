## What it solves

Alpha Screener is a research and decision-support workspace for examining market opportunities with visible confidence, uncertainty, and evidence. It helps a person decide what to inspect next rather than presenting opaque recommendations or automated actions.

## Key decisions and tradeoffs

<DecisionBlock kind="decision" title="Keep deterministic scoring separate from advisory context">
  Deterministic scoring remains available independently of optional advisory context. Advisory context can be suppressed when trust is invalid, confidence is low, market conditions are stressed, or a user preference requires it.
</DecisionBlock>

<DecisionBlock kind="tradeoff" title="Treat research state as evidence, not permission">
  Proxy-versus-full analysis differences are labeled explicitly, and exploratory, candidate, and proof-ready research states stay distinct. This prevents a promising research result from being presented as ready for action.
</DecisionBlock>

## Validation

Focused tests cover uncertainty, readiness, and signal-trust behavior, including insufficient-sample blocking, non-positive-edge rejection, readiness alignment, cost-eliminated candidates, and signal-promotion guardrails. Source inspection also confirmed server-side validation and read-only research modules.

<DecisionBlock kind="limitation" title="Research evidence has practical limits">
  Market data can be incomplete, stale, provider-dependent, derived, or proxy-based. The system is not investment advice, a vendor-certified model, a live-order execution system, or a performance claim.
</DecisionBlock>

## Human value

The workspace is intended to reduce decision overload by showing a ranked starting point, the evidence to inspect next, uncertainty signals, and clear invalidation context instead of hiding uncertainty behind a recommendation.
