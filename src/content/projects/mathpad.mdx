## What it solves

MathPad explores a local-first calculation workspace for arithmetic, variables, units, functions, and date or time expressions across durable sheets and working copies.

## Who it helps

It is designed for people working through multi-line calculations who need readable results, units, context, and error feedback to remain connected as the document changes.

## Design choice and tradeoff

A revisioned semantic document is shared through library-neutral editor ports, separating workspace-document state from the calculation-engine session. The design prioritizes coherent parsing, diagnostics, result alignment, and accessible state communication over unsupported claims of parity.

## Validation boundary

The repository defines lint, automated-test, stress-test, build, and dependency-audit gates, with coverage for keyboard navigation among recent tests. Inventory review did not rerun the full release gate, and characterization evidence does not establish complete compatibility with another calculation product.
