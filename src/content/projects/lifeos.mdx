## What it solves

Intentions, commitments, and time brought into a workable view. LifeOS is a human-centered workspace for aligning desired activity with the time actually available, while externalizing the changing state around that plan so the next action does not depend on fragile working memory.

## Who it helps

It is designed for people who need to make intentions workable alongside real commitments and limited time, especially when plans and context change across interruptions.

## Design choice and tradeoff

The architecture extends existing state and synchronization surfaces through explicit domain contracts rather than adding a replacement orchestrator or persistence model. Consequential or ambiguous automation is represented as a user-confirmed intervention with a safe timeout disposition.

## Validation boundary

The inspected codebase has typed state contracts, local-first persistence and synchronization surfaces, and a verified type check. It does not establish production deployment, cross-device reliability, accessibility outcomes, or completed end-to-end scenarios.
