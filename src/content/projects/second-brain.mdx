## What it solves

Second Brain is a durable context layer for preserving session continuity and recoverable handoffs across complex work. It reduces the need to reconstruct prior decisions, unresolved constraints, and the next useful action after an interruption.

## Who it helps

It is designed for people moving through long-running technical or creative work where the important state lives across sessions, artifacts, and repositories rather than in one short task.

## Design choice and tradeoff

Durable context remains a distinct responsibility. Second Brain records cycle state and handoff-oriented artifacts without becoming the runtime, governance authority, coordinator, or source of code truth. That boundary favors inspectable continuity over claims of autonomous memory.

## Validation boundary

Source inspection confirmed repository-owned contract, preflight, smoke, latest-cycle, session-board, and handoff surfaces. This review did not run live writes, remote-host checks, browser flows, or retention and privacy validation, and it does not establish production reliability.

## Next step

Run the repository-owned contract checks against a clean revision, then validate one direct durable-write and re-entry scenario without exposing private operational material.
