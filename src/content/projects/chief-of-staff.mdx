## What it solves

Chief of Staff explores a deliberately thin control plane for coordinating durable AI-assisted software work without replacing the systems that govern runtime, memory, or source code.

## Who it helps

It is designed for people coordinating work across projects and workers who need progress, ownership, and recovery state to remain reviewable when execution is uncertain, blocked, or handed off.

## Design choice and tradeoff

Typed lifecycle states make admission, execution, verification, human review, and handoff observable. Repository guards and independent verification prevent a worker-reported result from becoming completion by itself. The tradeoff is intentional: the system coordinates existing authorities instead of centralizing them.

## Validation boundary

Deterministic fixtures and offline tests cover normal completion, stale leases, human-waiting decisions, recovery, and handoff projections. This is not evidence of live runtime integration, fleet operation, or completed releases; those remain explicit next validation steps.

## Next step

Validate the prepared live-admission boundary only with explicit operator acknowledgement and preserve recoverable states when work is uncertain or blocked.
