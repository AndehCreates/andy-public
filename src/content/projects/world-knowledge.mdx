## What it solves

World Knowledge provides a durable reference layer for research and system-design work without collapsing source intake, curation, and retrieval into a single trusted channel.

## Who it helps

It is designed for people and AI-assisted workflows that need reference material to retain visible provenance, review boundaries, and a clear path for human judgment.

## Design choice and tradeoff

The corpus keeps candidate material, curated records, and bounded read-only retrieval distinct. That makes the reference layer easier to inspect, while leaving source verification and curation deliberately unfinished where evidence is incomplete.

## Validation boundary

The reviewed source's strict validation completed without strict warnings at the selected revision. That check covers structural rules such as frontmatter, relations, links, and placeholder language; it does not establish citation completeness, corpus quality, retrieval quality, or real-world outcomes.

## Next step

Continue source verification and human curation before expanding the corpus's public role or making stronger claims about its contents.
