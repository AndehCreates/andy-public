## What it solves

Fable is a collection of self-contained interactive explanations. Each page takes one idea and builds an explorable simulation around it, making the relationship between the model and the explanation visible.

## Who it helps

It is designed for readers who want to examine an idea by interacting with it instead of receiving only a static description.

## Design choice and tradeoff

Each explanation stays small and self-contained, keeping its controls, simulation, and explanatory surface close together. That bounds each page to one idea and keeps the collection focused rather than presenting it as a general-purpose platform.

## Validation boundary

Source-owned checks at the reviewed head report 24 sites, 61 edges, and 26 pages, with generated output and page metadata in sync. These checks establish structural consistency and generated-file freshness; they do not establish audience comprehension, adoption, performance, or production reliability.
