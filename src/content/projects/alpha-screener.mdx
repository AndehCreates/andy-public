## What it solves

Alpha Screener is a research and decision-support workspace for examining market opportunities with visible confidence, uncertainty, and evidence instead of opaque recommendations.

## Who it helps

It is designed for a researcher deciding what to inspect next when inputs may be incomplete, stale, provider-dependent, derived, or proxy-based.

## Design choice and tradeoff

Deterministic scoring remains separate from optional advisory context. The system labels proxy-versus-full analysis differences and uses explicit evidence gates so exploratory research is not presented as trade-ready.

## Validation boundary

Focused tests cover uncertainty, readiness, and signal-trust rules, including insufficient-sample blocking and invalid readiness alignment. Market data can be incomplete, stale, provider-dependent, derived, or proxy-based; this is not investment advice, live-order execution, or a performance claim.

## Next step

Extend bounded validation around live-data conditions and outcome research without weakening human review or evidence gates.
