## What it clarifies

Nexus defines the governance responsibility in the wider system: the conditions, accountability, and evidence required before consequential automated work advances. It is presented as a reviewed governance design, not as a verified deployed service.

## Who it helps

It is intended for people overseeing automated or agent-assisted work who need approval, execution, and verification responsibilities to remain attributable and reviewable.

## Design choice and tradeoff

Governance remains separate from coordination and execution. Chief of Staff may reconcile work, runtime systems may admit and execute it, and repositories may retain code truth, but none of those responsibilities silently becomes the authority that defines its own approval conditions.

## Validation boundary

The surrounding architecture consistently names Nexus as the governance and accountability boundary. No Nexus-owned source, test, command, deployment, or outcome evidence is currently available in the portfolio inventory, so this page makes no implementation, reliability, scale, or operational claim.
